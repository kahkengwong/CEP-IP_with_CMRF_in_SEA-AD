  GUIDE TO CMRF (Copula Monotone Recovery Filter):

  CMRF operates in (u,v) GOI-cDFG copula space. Two-stage filter:

  STAGE 1 -- Monotone Band Admission:
  Stage 1 (spine proximity + adaptive delta): |v_i - g(u_i)| <= delta_adapt(u_i)
    g(u): mgcv spine, donor RE + inverse-donor weights [PRSS, 8+5 iter];
          non-decreasing is verified per fit (GR-10), not imposed.
    delta_adapt(u) = delta_base*(1+kappa*K(u,IP_u,bw)).
    delta_base = weighted quantile(|v - g(u)|, 0.95), all original SKCS-core.
    delta is a RESIDUAL TOLERANCE -- NOT Kendall tau (Ki). No circularity.
    The adaptive band follows the diagonal spine; any cell within delta_adapt
    is geometrically consistent with the monotonic pattern.  No hard arm-
    concordance cutoff imposed -- one geometric criterion (band proximity), one statistical
    criterion (RSk-fCV).  Clean, consistent, immune to circularity.

  STAGE 2 -- Isotonic Fidelity Gate (paired Wilcoxon, iterative removal):
    Pool = OriRef + active candidates. Standard K-fold CV on the pool.
    RSk-fCV: K=5 folds x 40 repeats = 200 total fold-RMSEs; stratified by u-quantile decile.
    Curve A: OriRef-only CV, folds from OriRef, computed once -> RMSE_A.
    Curve B: pool CV, folds from the pool, recomputed each iteration -> RMSE_B.
    Matched by (repeat, fold) index. dRMSE therefore carries the candidates'
    effect on the fit, their presence in Curve B's test set, and Curve B's
    larger training set (B10E-31).
    Tests collective isotonic coherence: does the union form a coherent monotonic group?
    Paired Wilcoxon (H1: RMSE_B > RMSE_A, alpha=0.05) + BV effect-size gate; structurally matched fold pairs.
    If RMSE_B significantly worse: remove candidate with highest PAVA isotonic residual, repeat.
    PAVA removal ranking decouples Stage 2 removal from spine admission (Stage 1).
    Stops when p>=0.05 OR dRMSE within the background variability BV (dual gate).
